<div class="join-page" markdown="1">

We're looking for curious, driven people at every stage — from undergraduates to postdoctoral fellows — to help us understand how lipids shape the cells that build the embryo. If that sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.

Email **michaelpiacentino [at] jhmi [dot] edu** with your CV/resume and a short note about your interests — whether you're looking to join the lab, collaborate, or just want to talk lipids and EMT.

### Graduate students

Looking for an outstanding PhD program? Mike is a faculty member in both [BCMB](https://bcmb.bs.jhmi.edu/) and [CMM](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/som/education-programs/graduate-programs/programs) at Hopkins, and we're actively recruiting graduate students through either program. CMM also offers a Master's track for those interested.

### Postdoctoral fellows

We welcome postdoctoral fellows with interests in lipid metabolism, membrane biophysics, or neural crest cell biology — reach out with your CV and a short note about your research interests and career goals.

### Undergraduates

Johns Hopkins undergraduates interested in developmental biology are always welcome to join the lab and learn chick embryology and live imaging firsthand — get in touch to learn more.
